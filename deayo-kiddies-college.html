Our History

A Citadel of Learning Since 2001

De Ayo International College is situated at Alakia, Old Ife Road, Ibadan, Oyo State. Established on 14th July 2001, it was dedicated by the Vice Chancellor of the University of Ibadan, Prof. Ayodele Falase, Bishop Wale Oke, and Governor Alh. Lam Adesina.

  • Started with just 11 students and 6 dedicated teachers; now a full-scale institution
  • Oyo State Government approved on 15th August 2001
  • Nursery and Primary section established in 2010 to build stronger foundations
  • Science Olympiad champions — three students topped the national list
  • Students exposed to Local, State, National, and International competitions
  • Science, Arts, and Commercial departments all operational
